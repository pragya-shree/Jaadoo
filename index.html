Meet the visitor from beyond

Jaadoo is a curious, funny and innocent alien who landed on Earth with one big mission: figure out how humans work. He doesn’t understand why you say “I’m fine” when you aren’t, why birthdays need candles, or why everyone waves at screens.

He isn’t here to conquer anything. He’s here to learn, laugh and make a friend.
